# Editing the La Soirée website

**Editor:** https://la-soiree-bridal.netlify.app/admin/ (will become lasoireebridal.com/admin/ once the domain is switched)
**Sign in:** click **Sign in with GitHub** and use the GitHub login you were given.
**Publishing:** every **Save** goes live automatically in about 1–2 minutes. Refresh the site to see it.

## The 5 everyday jobs

**1. Add a new gown**
Gowns → **New Gown** → type the name, pick the designer and silhouette → under Photos click **Add** and upload (first photo = cover; drag to reorder) → **Save**.
Its page appears at /gowns/gown-name/ and in the Collection.

**2. Remove a gown that has sold or left the salon**
Gowns → open it → tick **Hide from site** → Save. (Untick to bring it back. Use Delete only if it's gone for good.)

**3. Change prices, hours, phone or links**
Settings → **Business details** (phone, email, address, hours, Square links)
Settings → **Appointment menu & prices** (keep in sync with Square)

**4. Write a journal post**
Journal → **New Post** → title, web address (e.g. `veil-styling-tips`), date, a 1–2 sentence summary, cover photo, then write the article. Tick **Draft** to save without publishing.

**5. Edit the FAQ or testimonials**
Settings → **FAQ** or **Testimonials**. Use `**double asterisks**` for bold. Quote reviews word for word.

## Photo tips
- JPG or PNG (iPhone HEIC photos: change the camera setting to "Most Compatible", or export as JPG first).
- Portrait photos (taller than wide) at least 1200 px wide look best for gowns.
- File names don't matter — the site renames and resizes everything automatically.

## If something looks wrong
Nothing can break the design from the editor. Every change is saved in history, so any edit can be undone. Contact Asean with the page link and what changed.
