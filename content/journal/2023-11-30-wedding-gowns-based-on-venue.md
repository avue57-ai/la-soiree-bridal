**Beach Wedding Dress** – if you’re having a beach wedding, you’ll want a dress that is light, airy, strapless or thin straps with little-to-no-train. With the wind and the sand combined at the beach, you want the least amount of hassle!

**Our pick:** the Marissa dress – one of our most light-weight gown in the boutique with a sheer lace bodice with a deep v-neck. The dress has no train but is still very bridal with its flared out tulle skirt.

![](/media-source/blog/2023_11_2023-11-29_0001.jpg)

**Forest/Wooded/Fairy wedding dress** – we’re imagining a very intimate setting surrounded by tall pine trees with fairy lights hung all around. To keep the vibe somewhat whimsical & mystical, you’ll want an A-line silhouette as you stroll around the forest and greet your guests in the evening.

**Our pick:** the Madie dress – an off-the-should gown with balloon sleeves and tulle skirt.

![](/media-source/blog/2023_11_2023-11-29_0002.jpg)

**Garden wedding dress** – think pastel and soft colors with delicate floral lace so as to not compete with the garden setting. For photos, you’ll want your dress to stand out so we recommend getting one with a blush or champagne underlay.

**Our pick:** The Elle dress – an corset A-line tulle gown with romantic off-the-shoulder straps and just enough florals embellished on to the bodice. The lace cascades perfectly and and fades into the skirt.

![](/media-source/blog/2023_11_2023-11-29_0005.jpg)

**Country club wedding dress** – clean, fitted and stream line with luxurious fabrics.

**Our pick:** The Jess dress – an off-the-shoulder fitted gown with an optional add-on overskirt made with luxurious mikado silk with just enough sheen.

![](/media-source/blog/2023_11_2023-11-29_0004.jpg)

**Barn or backyard wedding dress** – you can pretty much get away with wearing anything you want for a barn or backyard wedding but we recommend going with a lace dress to add some “wow” factor to your overall look.

**Our pick:** the Coleen dress – a fitted lace dress with a touch of subtle sexy.

![](/media-source/blog/2023_11_2023-11-29_0006.jpg)
