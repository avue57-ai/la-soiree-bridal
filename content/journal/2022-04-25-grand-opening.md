We’re so excited to announce that La Soiree Bridal will be opening its doors on September 1, 2022.

Our grand opening will feature a full blown fashion show featuring over 50 new wedding dresses from the boutique. Be ready for LOTS of prettiness, bubbly champagne, fun music and delicious hors d’oeuvres! Stay tuned for more details to come!

La Soiree Bridal is west Michigan’s newest bridal boutique and wedding dress shop located in Kalamazoo, Michigan. We carry brands such as Eva Lendel, Madi Lane and our very own private collection, SOIREE. How are we different from other boutiques? We offer brides and their loved ones an intimate shopping experience where you get the whole boutique to yourself and have free rein to select any dresses that you want to try on. We’ve also curated a collection of gowns that are unique and on trend and can’t wait to showcase all of our beautiful gowns and celebrate this special moment with our brides!

La Soiree Bridal is located at 8880 N 32nd street, Suite #1, Richland, Michigan 49083. The 3000 square foot boutique has several dressing rooms with a beautiful and relaxed lounge area for brides and their loved ones to enjoy. The bridal shop offers brides and elevated shopping experience in an intimate setting. Each bride will have a dedicated bridal stylist to help her get in and out of the wedding gowns. La Soiree Bridal is open by appointment only and each appointment will last approximately 90 minutes. Each bride is allowed to bring up to 4 guests for their bridal appointment. During your bridal appointment, you can expect to try on between 8-12 dresses (most of which is selected by you and some selected by our bridal stylists).
