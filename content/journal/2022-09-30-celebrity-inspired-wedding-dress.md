Today’s post is going to be a fun one as we find gowns from our bridal boutique that are similar to celebrities’ wedding dress.

1) We’ve matched **Hailey Beiber**‘s beautiful wedding dress with one of Eva Lendel’s best seller: the **Aretta** dress. Both gowns are off the shoulders with sleek long sleeves made with a corset bodice and a fitted bottom. The Aretta dress also comes with an overskirt so you can have a 2-in-1 look (wear the overskirt during the ceremony and take it off during the reception).

The second challenge is **Paris Hilton’s** infamous wedding dress which we’ve paired with Wona Concept’s **Coco** gown: a high neck lace gown with long sleeves and tulle bottom. This is probably one of the most iconic gowns that we think will remain classic forever!

We know it’s tough to match **Kate Middleton’s** dress but we took a slightly different spin to it and matched it with the Eva Lendel **Helly** gown: a two-piece satin gown with a long sleeve body suit. The fabric on this gown is just buttery soft and would work for a winter or spring wedding!

We absolutely love **Sofia Vergara’s** dress withthe overskirt and matched it with our very own Lika dress from Eva Lendel: a fitted sleeveless dress with a detachable overskirt. Every bride who’s tried on the Lika dress falls in love with it!

We couldn’t resist Issa Rose’s wedding dress and paired with Luce+Sposa’s Abigail’s ballgown: a tiered tulle ballgown made with the softest chiffon fabrics.

Yes Elsa is considered a celebrity and we couldn’t help but feature the Eva Lendel **Alisia** gown: an off-the-shoulder A-line gown with a layer of glitter tulle.

This was such a fun challenge and we’ll be doing more of this in the future! Let us know how we did!
