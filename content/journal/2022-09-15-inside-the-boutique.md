La Soiree Bridal is officially opened as of September 1, 2022 and we would love to welcome you to our wedding dress boutique here in Kalamazoo, Michigan. It’s been a long summer with us renovating and furnishing the boutique but we have to admit: it was quite fun and we wouldn’t have it any other way!

A little background about our bridal boutique: The name “La Soiree” means a fancy affair and stemmed from our vision to create an intimate and beautiful space for our brides to have an intimate wedding dress shopping experience with their closest family members and friends.

So how are we different from all the other bridal shops? We carry European style gowns and are the only bridal shop in Michigan that carries [Eva Lendel](/designers#eva-lendel), [Wona Concept](/designers#wona) and [Luce](/designers#luce-sposa)[+Sposa](/designers#luce-sposa) (some of the most talented European wedding dress designers in the world). We also carry a selection of little white dresses which are perfect for rehearsal dinners, bachelorette parties and reception dancing. Other merchandise in our boutique includes: veils, bridal jewelry, ring boxes, vow booklets and other fun and unique bridal accessories.

We are also a woman-owned boutique and we are from this area so the local community means a lot to us. We’re excited about the journey ahead and would love for you to follow us on [Instagram](http://www.instagram.com/lasoireebridal) where we are the most active.

We hope to see you soon in our boutique; in the meantime, here are some sneak peek of the boutique:
